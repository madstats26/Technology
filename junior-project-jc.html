Junior Cycle project

3rd year. The SEC sets the brief, and you design, make and document a solution. It is worth 70% of your Applied Technology grade. 2027 deadline: 30 April.

The project at a glance

70%

Of your final grade

Set and marked by the State Examinations Commission (SEC). The written exam is the other 30%.

3rd

Year

You start the project after Classroom-Based Assessment 2 (CBA 2), your self-analysis in 3rd year, term 1.

How it fits together

  1. CBA 2: look back at your coursework skills, find your strengths and what to improve. Use the CBA planners on the Junior Cycle page.
  2. The brief: choose one of the three 2027 briefs: a theatre stage prop, an animal welfare device, or a charity coin collector. Agree your direction with your teacher.
  3. Design and make: work through the steps, keeping your learning log up to date every week.
  4. Hand in: your artefact and My Design Journey folio must be ready for assessment by 30 April 2027. Check your teacher's own deadline, which may be earlier.
Your teacher's instructions, the SEC brief and its deadlines always come first. This section is a guide.

Choose one of three briefs

Every brief needs an electro-mechanical solution with a control system of your choice, and it must be aesthetically pleasing.

1. Theatre stage prop

Behind every breath-taking moment on a modern theatre stage lies a hidden world of electro-mechanical prop design. Stage props are the portable objects actors interact with to bring a live performance to life.

Task: in the context of a particular theatrical show, design and make an electro-mechanically operated prop that can be reused for successive performances of that show.

Your solution may be a scaled model, or full size if it is a smaller artefact.

2. Animal welfare

Animal welfare means making sure animals under human care live healthy, comfortable, stress-free lives, with proper nutrition, safe environments and the chance to express their natural behaviours. Hygiene, feeding systems and environmental controls are all part of animal care.

Task: in the context of an animal or animals of your choice under human care, design and make an electro-mechanical device or system that caters to an essential need of your selected animal(s).

Your solution may be a scaled model, or full size if it is a smaller artefact.

3. Charity coin collection device

Charities often rely on public coin collection devices as a simple, accessible way for people to donate their spare change.

Task: in the context of a charity of your choice, design and make a fun, interactive coin collection device with a suitable electro-mechanical feature. It should be designed for the countertop of a shop, or as a wall fixture in a shop.

Rules you must follow

  • It must be your own individual work, completed in school under your teacher's supervision. Your artefact must never leave the school.
  • Include your original sketches in the folio.
  • Show a variety of manual processing skills, not just CAD/CAM. Where you use CAD, CAM or control technology, back it up in the folio with drawings, flowcharts, program code or photos.
  • House circuits and controls neatly, and make them easy to reach for the examiner.
  • Power supply: no more than 12 V DC.
  • Include primary research where it fits.
  • Acknowledge every source, including websites and anything made with AI. Copied material gets no marks.
  • Write your examination number clearly on both your artefact and your folio.
