Project guides

1st year: Bedside lamp

Design and make a bedside lamp from recycled materials, using electronics and CAD.

Skills: Design process, electronics (LED circuit), CAD, materials, tools and safety.

Steps

Tip: Check the circuit has a resistor in series with the LED. Test the circuit before you fix it inside the lamp.
1st year: Build a bridge

Design, build and test a bridge that carries a load.

Skills: Structures and forces, triangulation, testing.

Steps

Tip: A strong bridge puts the material where the forces are. Record the load it held and what failed first.
2nd year: Cams project

Design and make a cam-driven model.

Skills: Cams and followers, mechanisms, making, CAD.

Steps

Tip: Small errors in the cam shape give jerky movement. Check that the follower stays in contact with the cam.
2nd year: Mechanisms project

Design and make a mechanism that uses gears, pulleys or linkages.

Skills: Gear ratios, pulleys, linkages, mechanical advantage.

Steps

Tip: Show your ratio calculation: ratio = driven teeth ÷ driver teeth. Test the real speed and compare.
3rd year: Junior Cycle project

Skills: The full design process, planning, making, testing, evaluating and documenting.

Steps

Tip: Keep your learning log up to date every week. Evidence of your thinking and changes is as important as the finished project.

Learning log

A learning log is your record of the project from idea to finished product. It is the evidence of your thinking. Keep it up to date as you go, not at the end.

What to record every lesson

  • The date and what you set out to do
  • What you did, with a sketch or photograph
  • What went wrong and how you fixed it
  • The decision you made and why
  • What you will do next

Make your evidence strong

  • Label every sketch and photograph.
  • Show your calculations and testing results.
  • Show iteration: the first version, what you changed, and the improved version.
  • Record your sources when you research.
  • Finish with an honest evaluation against the brief.

Useful sentence starters

  • I chose this idea because…
  • When I tested it, I found…
  • To improve it, I changed…
  • If I did it again, I would…
